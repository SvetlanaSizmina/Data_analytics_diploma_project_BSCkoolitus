Andmepõhine e-poe ostukäitumise ja kliendibaasi analüüs probleemide ja arenguvõimaluste tuvastamiseks müügi suurendamise eesmärgil

Translated Title (English):

Data-Driven Analysis of E-commerce Purchasing Behaviour and Customer Base to Identify Business Challenges and Growth Opportunities for Increasing Sales

📌 Executive Summary
A real-world e-commerce data analytics diploma project completed as part of my studies at BCS Koolitus. The project analysed sales trends from 2020 to 2026, customer purchasing behaviour and repeat purchases, abandoned shopping carts and their value, and customer base development over time. It applied RFM analysis and customer segmentation to identify key customer groups. The aim was to identify business challenges, uncover growth opportunities, and develop data-driven recommendations to increase sales.

🛠️ Tools & Tech Stack
SQL & Data Modeling (DBeaver / PostgreSQL): Checked data quality, cleaned and prepared data for analysis, created fact and dimension tables, and prepared datasets for use in Python and Power BI.

Python (VS Code): Analysed sales and customer data to understand purchasing behaviour, compare one-time and repeat customers, track purchase frequency, analyse shopping carts, and explore customer base growth and RFM segments.

Power BI: Created interactive dashboards and visualisations for the entire analysis, covering sales trends, year-on-year comparisons (2026 vs. 2025), completed and cancelled orders, average order value, product sales, customer activity, and RFM customer segments.

Excel: Used for initial calculations, organising the analysis process, checking data, and performing additional analysis when needed.
